Our Story

A Legacy of Design Excellence

Founded in 2012, MSI Elite Design Studio began with a simple yet powerful vision — to transform ordinary spaces into extraordinary experiences. What started as a two-person studio in Banjara Hills has grown into one of Hyderabad's most sought-after interior design firms.

Over the past 12 years, we have successfully completed more than 500 projects ranging from cozy apartments to sprawling corporate offices. Our design philosophy blends timeless elegance with modern functionality, ensuring every space we touch becomes a masterpiece.

Today, we are a team of 40+ talented designers, architects, project managers, and craftspeople united by our passion for exceptional design.

Our Journey

Company Timeline

1

2012 — Founded

MSI Elite Design Studio was established in Banjara Hills, Hyderabad with a two-person team and a dream to redefine interior design.

2

2014 — First 50 Projects

Completed our first 50 residential projects and expanded our team to 10 members. Won 'Emerging Design Studio' award.

3

2016 — Commercial Expansion

Entered the commercial design space with our first corporate office project. Revenue crossed ₹5 Crore.

4

2018 — 200+ Projects Milestone

Crossed 200 projects, expanded to a 4,000 sq.ft. studio, and partnered with leading material brands.

5

2021 — Industry Recognition

Won 'Best Interior Design Firm in South India' award. Team grew to 35+ professionals.

6

2024 — 500+ Projects & Growing

500+ completed projects, 300+ happy clients, and expanding to Bengaluru and Mumbai. The journey continues.
